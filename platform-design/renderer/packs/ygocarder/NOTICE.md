[`975387bc89a917e4422add3ece85f48170182ce6`](https://github.com/lauqerm/ygocarder/tree/975387bc89a917e4422add3ece85f48170182ce6).

The pinned [upstream README](https://github.com/lauqerm/ygocarder/blob/975387bc89a917e4422add3ece85f48170182ce6/README.md)
answers “Can I use your source code in my site?”:

> Yes, you are free to fork it and do whatever, just don't claim it is yours.
> Also, I will be extra thankful if you keep the credit for me and the template makers.

It separately asks users who want to modify assets to contact the template makers.
All 67 PNG/WOFF2 files included here retain their original upstream bytes. The
renderer composes them as image layers; it does not rewrite the source files.
This project does not claim ownership of these templates or assign a new license
to them. No SPDX license is reported by the upstream repository.

`provenance.json` records the pinned source URL/path, Git blob SHA-1, SHA-256,
byte count and image dimensions for every file. Tests verify these hashes locally.
`manifest.json` describes composition/placement on the 813 × 1185 canvas, including
separate Pendulum variants. Placement is informed by the upstream coordinate data;
these are not screenshots certified against DuelingBook's current renderer.

Included: 9 base frames, 9 name textures, 9 effect textures, 7 Pendulum frame
layers, 7 Pendulum text textures, 5 border layers, 7 monster attributes,
2 Spell/Trap badges, 8 icons, 2 Link arrow layers and 2 WOFF2 fonts. There are 15 full-card frame composites
plus Link-arrow and Pendulum-border composites. Card illustrations are not included.

Keep this notice and `provenance.json` with copies of the pack.
