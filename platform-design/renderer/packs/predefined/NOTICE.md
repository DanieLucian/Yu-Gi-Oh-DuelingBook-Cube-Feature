# User-supplied card frames

These ten PNG files were supplied by the repository owner in
`ygo-renderer-card-frames.zip` for use by this renderer. They are stored byte for
byte as received, at their original 549 × 800 dimensions. They have not been
recolored, cropped, repainted or attributed to the wiki or YGO Carder.

`provenance.json` records the archive SHA-256 and each original file's SHA-256,
byte size and dimensions. The archive did not include an author or license
notice; this record does not assert authorship or grant additional rights.

Nine images are complete card frames. `frame-pendulum-large.png` is a transparent
overlay, composed over the selected monster frame. Their artwork and text boxes,
outer borders, and printed ATK/DEF/LINK labels are part of the original images.
Dynamic artwork, text, attribute/subtype icons, stars, scales and selected Link
arrows are drawn over the appropriate slots by the SVG renderer.

The default manifest is `../manifest.json`. Fonts, attributes and subtype/star
icons remain separately credited under [YGO Carder](../ygocarder/NOTICE.md).
The old layered pack is retained for compatibility but is no longer the default
frame source.
