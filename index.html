DuelingBook Cube Draft Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Developer: DanieLucian

Data used and stored

DuelingBook Cube Draft reads rendered card information from DuelingBook when you use its import and preview features. Your Cube is saved in your Chrome profile using chrome.storage.local. It may contain card names, IDs, descriptions and statistics, artwork and source-page URLs, copy counts, deck sections, timestamps and custom metadata.

Local processing and sharing

The extension does not upload your Cube to developer-operated servers, sell your data, or include analytics or advertising trackers. It does not require a separate extension account or use Chrome sync storage. JSON backups are exported to a file you choose to save. JSON imports and XML ID matching process files you select locally; those files are not uploaded by the extension. You control any backups you share.

Images and external services

Displaying card artwork and full-card previews can cause normal requests to DuelingBook or the original artwork/asset providers. Those services may receive ordinary network information such as your IP address and requested image URL, under their own privacy policies. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process website request information under its privacy statement.

Your choices

You can remove individual cards or use Clear local collection. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage. Previously exported backups remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

Developer and questions

The developer is DanieLucian. For privacy questions, use the developer support contact shown on the extension’s Chrome Web Store listing.
